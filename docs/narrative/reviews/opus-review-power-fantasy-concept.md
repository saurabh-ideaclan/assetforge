# Opus review: *The Weight He Carries* (doc 22)

Inputs: docs 22 and 20 (the research's craft claims are self-declared unverified; treated as hypotheses).

## 1. Verdict in five lines

1. Yes, build the pilot on it: the loan/Toll economy and a hero who pays other people's price make a genuinely original engine with built-in moral cost.
2. The family layer (assessor father, the nudge, the money, the shop bought with it) is the best thing in the document and is woven into the world's mechanics, not bolted on.
3. The power system has one accounting contradiction (is Weight a debt or a credit?) that will break scenes from chapter 5 on if it is not settled now.
4. The plot has several continuity errors a writer will trip on immediately (the night-job branch, Cato's timeline, "Settlement" meaning two things, the pilot's place rule).
5. **Single biggest risk:** the hero's power is invisible and passive in the pilot, and every secret in the city routes back to the Hale family, so it reads as neither power fantasy nor a big world.

## 2. Originality

**Genuinely its own (§2, §3, §9):** power as a loan paid in Toll; Bearers as hidden labour under both the Tithe and the state (the Settlement parallel is sharp); a father whose crime is *misreading*; Calling that carries the lender's memory.

**Leaning too hard:**
- **The retest.** Ch1 opens on "retest day" and the pilot's hook is the retest paper (§8). Re-measurement is a signature reference beat; research §6 bans "the hidden-class test reveal". Move the humiliation off a measuring ceremony (a Firm hiring line, a pay window that stamps NULL).
- **Weak hero trapped with a stronger hunter** (ch2a) echoes the reference's inciting trap. Survivable, but never stage it as a sealed room with a trial.
- **Calling vs. a summoned army.** Echoes of other people's powers can drift visually toward ghostly allies standing behind him. Make an art rule now: an echo manifests as the *effect* (stopped air, heat), never as a figure.
- **Firms / Registry / Measure** are guilds / association / ranks renamed, but Measure is genuinely reshaped (a borrowing limit tied to insurance and marriage). Acceptable.

No hard red flags: no interface, levels, shadows, or quest panel.

## 3. The power system

**Coherence.** The core loop (Bearing stores, Calling spends) is elegant and gives every display of strength a cost against future safety (§7 last paragraph). But:

- **Debt or credit?** §3.1 says Weight is "carried Toll" that shows on him and accumulates toward collapse: a debt. §3.3 says he can "spend stored Weight" to call a power, "and then that Weight is gone": a credit. If Calling *reduces* Weight, Calling is free relief, and the optimal play is to carry everything and call it all back. Decide: either the Toll is paid at once (he loses the morning) and what remains is a **claim** he can cash, with Weight as the physical strain of holding claims; or Weight is unpaid debt and Calling *adds* to it. Two quantities are cleaner than one.
- **When does he choose what to lose?** Ch3b says "Jun chooses what to lose"; §3.4 lists choosing what to pay with as *later* progression. Pick one.
- **"Settlement"** is both the prison (§2) and the body paying all at once (§3.1). Rename the collapse.
- **Rules needed by chapter 2:** range (touching? same room?); does the lender know; what makes a carry involuntary; does the born debt use capacity; can he refuse mid-use.
- **Timing:** §3.3 says Calling at "chapter 7–8", the table says 8.

**Is the second awakening prepared?** Partly. The ch1 reading and ch5 involuntary call are real plants; Idris's "door, not a wall" line is a fortune cookie. Add a rule-level plant: Idris once called a power and paid for it (why he retired), so Calling is known and feared before Jun does it.

**Support first, striker later** satisfies only if support moments are *decisive and witnessed* (research §2.1, §2.4); in the pilot only Idris and Penny see Bearing. Give it an active edge: if Jun can **let go** of a carry mid-use, the Toll lands on the user at once. That gives the Penny rescue a fair-play trick (take the collector's cost, then drop it) and makes a support power that strikes.

## 4. The father and the mother

**Tomas** is well built: a kind nudge, coercion, then years of paid complicity, with the shop bought from it (§4). That last detail makes the whole family complicit and is the strongest moral material in the document. Risks:
- **The protective Null reading excuses too much** if it lands as "he did it for you." It is also pulling against the other reveal (he helped put the debt in Jun), so the two could cancel into a plot device. Make the Null reading serve both love and self-preservation: a Null son is never re-read, so nobody finds the transfer Tomas helped do. One act, two motives; the reader sympathises and disagrees in the same scene (research principle 11).
- **"Confessed to the readings and nothing else"**: say whom the silence protects (the family? himself? Selene?). It is the question his visit scene turns on.
- **"T. Hale, dated last week"** is a great hook only if the answer is planned (he still performs readings inside Settlement? forged?). Write the answer before chapter 3.

**Mara** has voice and dignity (accounts at the table, collector chess). But her decisions are offscreen: secret visits, what she gave up "later". Her one on-page act (the no to Selene) has no visible cost. Show the cost in chapter 2 (she sells a tool, signs something, Jun sees the shelf empty). And decide whether she knows the shop was bought with dirty money; if she does, she has her own moral complexity and a reason to refuse Selene that is not pure pride.

## 5. The protagonist

The flaws (deciding for others "for their own good", taking the hit, lying to his mother) are excellent: they are his father's and they drive scenes. But ch1a introduces him as a victim. Readers bond with competence plus warmth before power: open on what he can do unpowered (he reads Seam-damaged machines better than anyone). The "ledger of slights" risks bitterness without humour, and §4 calls him "sharp in words" without one sample line; write five voice lines before outline approval. "One person at the Registry to look at him twice" is a lovely want; pay it off with a specific clerk.

## 6. Supporting cast

| character | person or slot | one improvement |

| Pike | slot ("the state's version of Selene") | give him a need: Settlement capacity is failing and Jun is the fix |

**Centre-of-the-universe check (research §4).** Tomas nudged Cato; Selene put the debt on Jun; Tomas helped; Idris was a Bearer; the Seam opens under Jun's yard; the assessor signature is his father's. Cut at least two of these links. The Tithe should have dozens of Bearers; Jun should be *one* case that matters to his family, not the hinge of every faction.

## 7. Choices

**Meaningful:** humiliation (the walk-out option changes how ch3 learns the anomaly; the fight is lost but witnessed); reveal/conceal (trust vs exposure); the father (three routes to different truths); whose cost to take; the memory to lose (if made concrete).

**Fake or broken:**
- **Money:** if Jun refuses the night job, why is he at the yard at night in ch1d? Both branches must reach the Seam (he goes back for his tools, or for a coworker who took the job).
- **Told Mara → she is at the yard:** ch2a is written for two people; have her arrive at dawn (ch2b) instead.
- **Humiliation "swallow it":** a `pride` cost is mentioned, but `pride` is not in the stat list.
- **Conceal path pilot payoff:** ch3d's win is "the first thing Jun has ever been chosen for", which only exists on the reveal path. Conceal needs an equal win (Penny chooses him; money for the shop).

**Stats:** nine numbers plus flags, against the research's four to six. `standing` ("slow") will not move in three chapters: bookkeeping. `capacity` is bookkeeping unless it gates options. A single `exposure` conflates the Tithe, the Registry and the Firms; research principle 18 says track knowledge per faction. Keep `weight` (it must show in art; budget the expression variants), trust for Mara/Idris/Noor, `father`, per-faction knowledge, and a list of lost memories. Add standing in chapter 4.

## 8. Season and pilot

**Season.** Chapters 4–7 risk a plateau: Bearing gets no upgrade between ch3 and ch8, and Calling at 8 of 12 leaves four crowded chapters for the new power. Move Calling to 7 (the father's truth as its trigger) or add a mid-season rung at ch4–5 (carrying for two; the let-go). Also, ch3's "visit" route duplicates ch7.

**Pilot rules contradict the scenes.** §8 says "one place change"; ch1 and ch3 each have four places, about 375 words per scene with 6–7 choices. Cut ch1 to three places (Mara brings the notice to the yard) and keep Pike and Oren off-screen.

**Does it deliver?** The ch1 hook (a whistle inside the Seam), ch2 hope and ch3 win-with-price plus signature hook are right. Missing: the *felt* payoff of power.

**Scene-level suggestions.**
- Ch1: show the caste in three small moments before the hall (a NULL stamp on a pay envelope, a tram priority line, an insurer refusing the shop); plant three specific memories (the sea with his father, Mara teaching him to solder, Noor's birthday).
- Ch2a: the carry is involuntary, but give the player one physical choice inside it (hold on to Idris or let go), which also plants the let-go rule.
- Ch2b: prove the lost morning by event: Mara mentions something from it and Jun cannot answer.
- Ch3b: the player picks which planted memory to pay; later lines that would reference it are removed or turn painful.
- Ch3: the win needs a witness who changes their mind (Noor or Idris sees the collector collapse).

## 9. Ten changes, ranked by impact

1. Settle the Weight accounting (paid Toll vs. cashable claim vs. bodily strain) so Calling is never free relief.
2. Give Bearing a witnessed, active edge in the pilot (the let-go) so support reads as power.
3. Cut at least two Hale-centred coincidences (the Cato nudge, Selene personally placing the debt) so the world is bigger than the family.
4. Fix the night-job branch so both money choices reach the Seam.
5. Fix Cato's timeline against "Measure fixed at eighteen", or drop the nudge link.
6. Make the memory cost a choice among memories planted in chapters 1–2 that later scenes honour.
7. Make Tomas's Null reading of Jun protect both his son and himself.
8. Give Mara an on-page decision with a visible cost in chapter 2, and decide what she knows about the money.
9. Move Calling to chapter 7, or add a Bearing upgrade at chapter 4–5.
10. Trim stats to six with per-faction knowledge, rename the collapse so "Settlement" means one thing, and move the humiliation out of a measuring ceremony.
